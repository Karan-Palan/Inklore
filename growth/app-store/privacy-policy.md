# Inkflow Privacy Policy

Inkflow is a reading and audiobook app that helps you build a lasting reading habit. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use Inkflow, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

By creating an account or using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of Inkflow.

## Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated by your use of the app, and technical data necessary to operate the service.

- Account information: your email address and display name, collected when you create an account via email/password, Sign in with Apple, or Continue with Google.
- Reading and listening activity: books you open, pages read, minutes spent reading or listening, streaks, and goals you set.
- Imported content metadata: titles and file names of PDF or EPUB files you import. File contents are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
- Notes and highlights: annotations you save within a book, which may be included in your daily email digest.
- Subscription and purchase information: records of active or past subscriptions processed through Apple's App Store.
- Usage and diagnostic data: app interactions, feature usage patterns, and crash reports collected to improve app performance.
- Email delivery data: the email address you designate for your daily notes digest, used solely to deliver that digest via Resend.

## How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- To authenticate your account and keep your session secure across devices.
- To sync your reading position, progress statistics, streaks, and goals.
- To generate AI-powered study notes from your reading content using OpenAI.
- To send your daily notes email digest to the address you provide.
- To process and validate your in-app subscription through Apple's StoreKit.
- To analyze aggregate usage patterns so we can improve features and fix bugs.
- To respond to support requests you submit.

## Product And Technical Data

We use Supabase as our backend provider for authentication, database storage, and Edge Functions. Your account credentials and reading data are stored in Supabase's infrastructure. Sessions are secured using tokens stored in your device's Keychain.

We use OpenAI's API to generate AI study notes from content you choose to process. Text submitted for note generation is transmitted to OpenAI subject to OpenAI's privacy and data-use policies.

We use Resend to deliver your daily notes email digest. Your designated email address is shared with Resend solely for the purpose of delivering that digest.

We use analytics tooling to collect anonymized, aggregated usage data. This data helps us understand how features are used and identify areas for improvement. It is not linked to your identity for advertising purposes.

We do not use advertising SDKs or share data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

## How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only in the limited circumstances described below.

- Service providers: Supabase (infrastructure and authentication), OpenAI (AI note generation), Resend (email delivery), and our analytics provider — each receiving only the data necessary to perform their function.
- Apple: subscription and purchase information is processed by Apple's App Store infrastructure.
- Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.
- Business transfers: if Inkflow is acquired or merged, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

## Data Retention

We retain your account and reading data for as long as your account remains active. If you sign out or stop using the app, your data remains stored until you request deletion.

To request deletion of your account and associated data, please contact us using the information in the Contact section below. We will process your request within a reasonable time frame and confirm when deletion is complete.

## Your Choices

You have the following choices regarding your data:

- Email digest: you can disable the daily notes email at any time within the app's settings.
- AI notes: submitting content to OpenAI for note generation is an action you initiate; you can simply not use the feature.
- Account data: you may request access to or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.
- Analytics: aggregate usage analytics help us maintain and improve the app. Specific opt-out options depend on your device's system settings.

## Children's Privacy

Inkflow is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us. Because certain contact details were not provided at the time this policy was generated, please refer to the support page within the Inkflow app for current contact information.

We will respond to privacy inquiries within a reasonable time frame.
