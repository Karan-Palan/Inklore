# Review Notes

- The app requires an account to use. You may create a test account using email and password from the sign-in screen — no invite code is needed.
- Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google are available on the sign-in screen. Email/password sign-up is accessible via the expandable section on the same screen.
- The daily email digest feature requires a valid email address entered in the app. It uses Resend for delivery.
- AI study notes are generated via OpenAI and require an active internet connection.
- Books can be discovered via the in-app search (Project Gutenberg / Internet Archive) or imported as PDF/EPUB from Files.
- Subscription unlocks additional features. Sandbox environment purchase testing is available with a Sandbox Apple ID.
- The app does not use the camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, or location services.
- No web URL for the app or legal entity was supplied — reviewer may note the absence of a marketing or privacy policy URL in metadata.
